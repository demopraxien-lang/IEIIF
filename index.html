About IEIIF

The Indo Entrepreneur and Industrial Innovation Foundation (IEIIF) is a not-for-profit organization incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, built to create a robust entrepreneurial ecosystem and strengthen India's industrial and MSME sector.

We believe entrepreneurship is one of the strongest pillars of national development — a progressive nation needs empowered entrepreneurs, skilled industries, financially literate business owners, innovative youth, and a workforce that continuously adapts to a changing economy.

Our work spans nurturing entrepreneurship from the school level, supporting startups, empowering existing MSMEs, promoting industrial innovation, and bridging industry with government to create sustainable industrial development across India.

Headquarters

Bengaluru, Karnataka

IEIIF aims to establish district-level industrial clusters throughout Karnataka, then progressively expand its network across every State and Union Territory of India.

Vision & Mission

What we're working toward, and how we get there.

Our Vision

An entrepreneurial India, globally competitive

To build an entrepreneurial India where innovation, industrial excellence, financial literacy, skilled manpower and collaborative growth create globally competitive enterprises — generating sustainable employment and inclusive economic prosperity.

Our Mission

Twelve commitments, one movement

  • Promote entrepreneurship as a national movement, beginning in schools, colleges and communities.
  • Strengthen India's MSME ecosystem through education, financial literacy and industrial mentoring.
  • Bridge industries, entrepreneurs, educational institutions and Government.
  • Establish district-wise industrial clusters for collaboration and business networking.
  • Contribute to Atmanirbhar Bharat through entrepreneurship-led economic development.

Sports Development Wing

Healthy industries need healthy people

Recognizing that healthy industries require healthy people, IEIIF has established a dedicated Sports Development Wing to promote wellness, leadership, teamwork and sporting excellence within industrial organizations — including programmes supporting para-athletes.
